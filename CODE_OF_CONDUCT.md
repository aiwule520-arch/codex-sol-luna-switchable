# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential exposure, and deliberate publication of another person's private data are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Critique claims, code, configuration, and evidence rather than people.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and safe.
